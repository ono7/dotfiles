## addons

**must haves**

- GearQuest forever - shows you where to find the next upgrade
- Dungeon Journal - shows you every boss and the loot table for each boss
- ForeverPlus - Auto pickup quest and auto turn in quest (we have a home brewed version of this)
  - can be setup to show latency, fps, gold and more things
- Attune - shows you what dungeons you are attuned to and what you need to get attuned
  - tells you what quests you need to be attuned to a dungeon

- forever quest tint - shows what quests are new in wow forever

**untested**

Dungeon Journal - shows all the dungeons, quests and rewards, and how to get the quests
Bigwigs - for boss encounter, warnings for raids and dungeons
moveany - move anything in the blizzard ui
trainer spell - tracks all class spells and where the trainers are as well as professions
rare scanner - scan rares
syndicator - track currency and gold accross characters
tomtom - shows coordinates
restedxp - paid for leveling guide
auctinator - need this for AH and selling
